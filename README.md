## Key Projects

### [AI vs. Human Decision-Making in Business Think Tanks](https://github.com/killianglavo-coder/Quantitative-and-Qualitative-Analysis-----LLMs-Comparison)
*MSc Major Project, Sep 2025 to Apr 2026*
Mixed-method research study comparing outputs from 11 human participants against four LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude) across three business scenarios and three prompt variants, scored on originality, usefulness, divergence, and convergence. Led the project end to end, including methodology design, participant coordination, and statistical testing for anchoring bias. Found that AI outputs consistently anchored on one or two answers regardless of prompt framing, while human responses stayed diverse. Translated findings into a tiered AI governance framework recommending multi-model ensembles and formal prompt-design protocols for business use.
`Statistical Analysis` `Qualitative Coding` `Research Design`

### [Rental Tier Classification: Irish Property Market](https://github.com/killianglavo-coder/Python-Machine-Learning----Rental-Tier-Classification-Irish-Property-Market)
*Python, scikit-learn*
Built a novel dataset by merging five public Irish data sources (RTB rent reports, Revenue county receipts, CSO population and income data, Teagasc land prices), engineering county-level features to classify residential properties into Budget, Standard, and Luxury tiers for a property development firm. Ran K-Means and Agglomerative clustering to test whether the tiers emerge naturally from the data, then trained and compared Logistic Regression and Random Forest classifiers, both reaching approximately 78% accuracy predicting tier without using rent price directly, showing genuine signal from county-level economic features alone.
`Python` `scikit-learn` `Clustering` `Classification` `Feature Engineering`

### [Decision Theory: Customer Segmentation](https://github.com/killianglavo-coder/Decision-Theory----Customer-Segmentation)
*Excel, RapidMiner, K-Means*
Analysed a large retail POS dataset from grocery store cashiers to extract customer purchasing insights for decision-making. Cleaned and structured the data in Excel (CONCAT, VLOOKUP, IFERROR, PivotTables), then applied K-Means clustering in RapidMiner to segment customers and baskets by category patterns, visualising cluster results for business interpretation.
`Excel` `RapidMiner` `K-Means Clustering` `Data Cleaning`

### [Database & SQL Query Design](https://github.com/killianglavo-coder/SQL-and-Database------Database-and-SQL-Query-Design-)
